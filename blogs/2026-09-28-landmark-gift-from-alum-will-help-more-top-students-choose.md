Landmark gift from alum will help more top students choose Queen’s jdr5 Mon, 09/28/2026 - 15:55 The $25-million endowed gift – the largest to student financial aid in Queen’s history – has established a new undergraduate award for incoming students with demonstrated financial need. The first recipients received support this fall. The new Ann Baker Tuition Award provides substantial support toward tuition and fees and is renewable for up to three additional years, giving recipients greater financial stability throughout their studies.
