Second-Hand Shuffle has record-breaking year jdr5 Tue, 08/04/2026 - 13:16 Over 7,000 items, including furniture, kitchenware, clothing, and household goods, were donated. Organizers estimate the event saved participants approximately $386,447 by reducing the need to buy new goods while avoiding over 124 metric tonnes of CO2 emissions. According to Natural Resources Canada, that is roughly equivalent to powering 115 homes for a year.
