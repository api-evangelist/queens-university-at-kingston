Queen’s launches new research administration system kvd1 Mon, 07/20/2026 - 14:37 Every research result, big or small, requires the behind-the-scenes work that enables research to happen: from applying for funds and formalizing partnerships to satisfying funders' reporting requirements, research administration is a core function of universities. Having effective support systems in place is essential to reduce administrative burden and foster research excellence. Researchers and research staff at Queen’s have a new tool for managing grants and contracts.
