Teaching excellence takes centre stage nld4 Wed, 10/14/2026 - 13:53 Principal and Vice-Chancellor Patrick Deane and Vice-Provost (Teaching and Learning) Gavan Watson hosted the awards reception in Grant Hall. The reception celebrated awards presented by the university, its faculties and schools, and student governments. Recipients were honoured for helping students make the most of their learning experiences and preparing them for the future, innovating the way courses are taught, and making the university environment more accessible and inclusive.
