Indigenous artists and stories take the stage at The Isabel nld4 Wed, 09/16/2026 - 18:23 An acclaimed opera about missing and murdered Indigenous women, a conversation with Cree author Michelle Good, and a harvest celebration led by Haudenosaunee artist David R. Maracle are coming to the Isabel Bader Centre for the Performing Arts this fall. Taking place from September 29 to October 2, the three events offer Queen’s and Kingston audiences distinct ways to experience Indigenous stories, knowledge, and artistic expression through opera, literature, and music.
