Sign up for QUAlerts emergency texts jdr5 Mon, 09/21/2026 - 15:31 Queen’s University is encouraging students, staff, and faculty who have not yet signed up for QUAlerts text notifications to register, so they receive critical information as quickly as possible in the event of a campus emergency. QUAlerts, the university’s emergency notification system, sends urgent messages through several channels. Email alerts to all Queen’s addresses are issued by default, while text alerts to your smartphone are available to those who opt-in.
