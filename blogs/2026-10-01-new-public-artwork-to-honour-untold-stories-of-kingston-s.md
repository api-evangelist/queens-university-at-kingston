New public artwork to honour untold stories of Kingston’s heritage jdr5 Thu, 10/01/2026 - 19:05 A new stainless-steel sculpture, Consonance, by Canadian artist Hooman Mehdizadehjafari, will be installed along the waterfront near the Isabel Bader Centre for the Performing Arts. The work was selected to honour and imagine a way forward for Kingston’s untold cultural heritage stories, and is expected to be completed and installed in December 2026. Consonance depicts a dynamic formation of diverse human figures rising and supporting one another toward a shared apex.
