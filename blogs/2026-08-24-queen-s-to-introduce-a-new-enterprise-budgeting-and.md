Queen's to introduce a new enterprise budgeting and forecasting tool jdr5 Mon, 08/24/2026 - 15:19 Queen’s is introducing OneStream, a new enterprise budgeting and forecasting platform designed to make budget development more consistent and streamlined. The platform is expected to launch in spring 2027 as part of the Queen’s Renew Program. Budget development at Queen’s currently relies heavily on Excel spreadsheets and manual data entry.
