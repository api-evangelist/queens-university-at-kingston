Building skills for success through experiential learning jdr5 Tue, 07/21/2026 - 18:00 Learning to set goals and having the confidence to achieve them is an important part of the university experience. While many of those goals are academic such as getting good grades and practicing effective study habits, others are wellness related such as getting more sleep or spending less time on a screen. Learning how to turn these goals into lasting habits is a skill that serves students well throughout their personal and professional lives.
