Ontario invests $104 million to expand undergraduate student seats at Queen’s jdr5 Wed, 10/14/2026 - 21:40 Today's funding announcement will support student seats in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM), health care, and education programs. The seats were allocated through a competitive provincial proposal process that evaluated institutional strengths, regional sector needs and student demand. Institutions worked with employers and community partners to determine where additional capacity could have the greatest impact.
